By default every click reaches the server: the card is charged twice. With 'drop', a click made while a charge is in flight makes mutate() reject with an AbortError whose message is 'Dropped', without calling mutationFn or touching the signals. In a real form, disabling the button while isPending() is the first line of defense; 'drop' also covers calls made from code.
