Both versions behave the same, and the hand-written one is correct. What rxMutation() removes is the bookkeeping: three signals, the guard, the flag reset in each branch and takeUntilDestroyed. It also gives input(), status() and reset() for free.

A dropped click makes mutate() reject with an AbortError ('Dropped'), without calling mutationFn or touching the signals. Disabling the button while isPending() is the first line of defense; 'drop' also covers calls made from code.
