Both behave the same: a second save while one is in flight is ignored, and leaving the page aborts the request. The hand-written version is correct. What rxMutation() removes is the bookkeeping: three signals, the guard, the flag reset in each branch and takeUntilDestroyed. It also gives input(), status() and reset() for free.
