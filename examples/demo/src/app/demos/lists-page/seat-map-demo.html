Both seat maps behave the same. By hand, the list of seats in flight is updated in three places: before the call, on success and on error; a branch that forgets it leaves a seat "Locking…" forever. With rxMutation(), pendingInputs() lists the inputs of the calls still in flight: a seat leaves it when its call settles, whatever the outcome, and reset() or destroy empty it. input() only follows the latest call, and isPending() only says whether any call is in flight: neither tells which seats are still locking.
