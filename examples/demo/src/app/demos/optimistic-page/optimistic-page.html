Showing a write before the server answers, without copying state: the pending calls are folded over the resource() in a computed(). A row is hidden while its delete is in pendingInputs(), a rename shows at once, and onSuccess writes the result into the resource. Source

Nothing to roll back: a failed call leaves pendingInputs(), so the row shows again in place, with its old name. Renames are queued (concurrency: 'queue') and folded in call order, so the latest wins on screen as on the server.

More patterns, such as a rollback that skips the writes that may have been applied, in the recipes.
