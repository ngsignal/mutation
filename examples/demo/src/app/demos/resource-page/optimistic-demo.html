A row is hidden while its delete is in pendingInputs(), and removed from the resource by onSuccess. A failed delete, as always for "Grace Hopper", just shows the row again, in place: nothing to roll back. Delete Grace, then quickly Ada.

A rename shows at once: the pending renames are folded over the resource, the latest wins. Each takes 2 s (an artificial delay). Rename Ada twice quickly, or clear a name: the server rejects it, and the old name shows again.
